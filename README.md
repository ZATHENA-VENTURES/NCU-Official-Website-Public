## ✨ Key Features & Pages

A full-featured university website covering degree browsing, course syllabi, giving, a bookstore, and student resources.

### 🎓 **Academics**
*   **Degree Programs**: All four levels laid out in full, from Associate of Arts through to Doctorate.
*   **Course Syllabi**: Every program lists its courses, with each syllabus readable directly on the site.
*   **Course Search**: Full-text search across every syllabus and page.
*   **Schedule & Downloads**: Class schedule, academic calendar, Student Handbook, Tuition & Fees, and the Course Catalogue.

### 🏛️ **About the University**
*   **Our Story**: The founding, mission, history, and core values of New Covenant University.
*   **Meet the President**: Dr. Angel L. Crites, Ed.D; D.Th, with full biography and credentials.
*   **Alumni Showcase**: Graduate stories and testimonials.
*   **Reviews**: Student and community reviews, pulled live onto the homepage.

### 💛 **Connect & Give**
*   **Donate**: Give through Zeffy, PayPal, Cash App, or Venmo, with confirmation and a record of your giving.
*   **Apply Now**: Admissions application, online.
*   **Bookstore**: Every title available as a donation gift of $15 or more, with cart and checkout.
*   **Contact**: Direct contact form, phone, email, mailing address, and campus locations.

### 🌐 **Site-Wide**
*   **Responsive Design**: Built for phone, tablet, and desktop alike.
*   **Dark / Light Mode**: System-aware, with your preference remembered.
*   **Accessibility Panel**: Font size, contrast, and motion controls from a floating widget.
